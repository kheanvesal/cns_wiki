# Reading Goals — Purpose × Depth Map

A cleaner frame for the four reading goals (see [[concepts/reading-goals-critique]]). Two underlying axes generate the space; the four current goals occupy four cells, and the empty cells are the scope gaps.

- **Purpose** (why you read): *understand · locate · verify · enjoy.*
- **Depth** (how much you process): *careful ↔ expeditious.*

## The map

| Purpose ＼ Depth | Careful (deep) | Expeditious (fast / selective) |
|---|---|---|
| **Understand** | **Comprehension** ✓ ([[syntheses/Comprehension]]) — + *learning-for-retention* (thin: [[sources/edmonds-2009-reading-interventions-synthesis]], now also [[sources/kreijkes-2026-llm-notetaking-comprehension]]) | **Skimming** ✓ ([[syntheses/Skimming]]) — gist / satisficing |
| **Locate** | *Evaluative / research reading* — **GAP** (relevance-judging while reading deeply; only touched: [[sources/rouet-2002-search-tasks-comprehension]], [[sources/papaeconomou-relevance-judgments-learning-style]]) | **Search / Scanning** ✓ ([[syntheses/Search]]) — split further in [[syntheses/skimming-vs-scanning-vs-search]] |
| **Verify** | **Proofreading** ✓ ([[syntheses/Proofreading]]) — surface-careful, error detection | *Quick spot-check* — **thin** (fast proofing under time pressure barely studied) |
| **Enjoy** | *Immersive / literary reading* — **GAP** (0 papers) | *Leisure browsing* — **GAP** |

✓ = a current hub · GAP = a reading goal the corpus does not cover.

## What the map reveals
- **The four goals are not a flat list** — they are four cells of a 4×2 space. Comprehension and skimming are the same *purpose* (understand) at two *depths*; that's why they share 25 sources ([[concepts/reading-goals-critique]]).
- **"Locate" spans depth** — quick scanning and careful evaluative reading are both locating, but only the fast end is a hub. The careful-locate cell (research/relevance reading) is largely empty.
- **"Enjoy" is entirely empty** — pleasure/leisure reading is unstudied in this corpus, matching the xlsx "reading goal = pleasure is essentially unstudied" gap.
- **Depth is a continuum, not a switch** — reading-to-summarize and learning-for-retention live *between* the careful and expeditious columns and have no clean hub.

## How to use it
- Treat the four hubs as **entry points**, not exhaustive bins; when a source straddles cells (56% do), file it under every cell it touches (multi-valued `reading_tasks`) and let the map explain the overlap.
- When sourcing new papers, the **GAP cells** are the highest-value targets: immersive/pleasure reading, careful evaluative reading, and fast spot-check proofreading.
