# Selective Attention

**What it is.** Directing reading attention to some text regions over others — the mechanism headings/signaling exploit and that skimming relies on.

**Key patterns.**
- Functional and content headings direct selective attention [[sources/functional-headings-selective-attention]] · [[sources/lemarie-lorch-perywoodley-2012-headings]].
- Skimming/foraging is selective allocation to high-value text [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]]; reading goal produces global attentional adjustment [[sources/reading-goal-task-eye-fixation-patterns]].
