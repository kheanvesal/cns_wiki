# Reading Strategies

**What it is.** Reader-side approaches to text — skim, scan, careful/deep read, reread at topic shifts — deployed based on goal and skill (distinct from typographic "style"; see [[sources/kirby-style-strategy-skill-reading]]).

**Key patterns.**
- Eye-fixation typologies reveal distinct reader strategies for summarizing [[sources/hyona-lorch-kaakinen-2002-reading-to-summarize]].
- Strategy interacts with skill and cannot be interpreted alone [[sources/kirby-style-strategy-skill-reading]]; interventions teach comprehension strategies [[sources/edmonds-2009-reading-interventions-synthesis]]; taught skim/scan strategies [[sources/tamsi-skimming-scanning-teaching-narrative]].
