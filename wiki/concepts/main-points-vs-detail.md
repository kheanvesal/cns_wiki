# Main Points vs. Detail

**What it is.** The distinction between gist/main-idea information and fine detail — the axis along which skimming's costs and benefits fall.

**Key patterns.**
- Skimming preferentially preserves **main points** while sacrificing detail [[sources/duggan-payne-2009-text-skimming]] · [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]].
- Speed–accuracy costs land more on detail than main-idea items [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2000-speed-patterns-screen]] — see [[concepts/question-type]].
- Signaling/headings help readers find high-value (main-point) regions [[sources/lemarie-lorch-perywoodley-2012-headings]].
