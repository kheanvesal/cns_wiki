# Reading Speed

**What it is.** Rate of reading (wpm or time-on-text). A behavioral measure that stands between legibility and comprehension: faster is not always better.

**Key patterns.**
- Larger font size raises speed without raising comprehension [[sources/ej1079769]]; spacing effects on speed are often null [[sources/interletter-line-spacing-reading-speed-comprehension]].
- Faster/skim reading trades off comprehension, unevenly across question types — see [[concepts/skim-comprehension-tradeoff]] [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2000-speed-patterns-screen]] · [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2001-speed-linelength-screen]].
- Disfluent fonts slow reading [[sources/font-disfluency-online-learning-highschool]]; search is ~2–2.5× faster than reading [[sources/fisher-1975-reading-visual-search]].
- Classic legibility optima are defined largely by speed [[sources/tinker-1963-legibility-of-print]]; applied speed-enhancement [[sources/abdullah-2018-reading-speed-hybrid-learning]].
