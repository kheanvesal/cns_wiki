# Columns

**What.** Multi-column text layout; effects interact with line length / characters-per-line.

**Evidence.**
- Column/window effects interact with cpl as the mediating variable [[sources/layout-on-screen]].
- **Century-scale review finds no consensus:** "almost equal numbers of studies showed advantages and disadvantages… as well as a preference of numbers of columns in text" [[sources/tarasov-legibility-of-textbooks-2015]].

**Pattern.** Columns are the layout variable most often studied and least often resolved. Because column count changes line length, any column effect is confounded unless cpl is held constant — and see [[concepts/methodology-critique]] for why cross-study comparison here is unreliable.
