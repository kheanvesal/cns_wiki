# Metacognitive Calibration

**What it is.** How accurately readers judge their own comprehension/performance. Miscalibration = feeling you understood more (or less) than you did.

**Key patterns.**
- Screen reading worsens calibration (overconfidence) vs. paper [[sources/clinton-2019-paper-vs-screens-meta]].
- Under time pressure, screen readers fail to adapt and mind-wander more, degrading both comprehension and self-monitoring [[sources/delgado-salmeron-2021-inattentive-onscreen-reading]].
- A key reason **preference/comfort** can mislead ([[concepts/method-type-divergence]]).
