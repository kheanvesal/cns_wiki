# Font Type / Typeface

**What it is.** The design of the letterforms (typeface/font family), including serif vs. sans and "disfluent" fonts.

**Evidence.**
- Typeface effects on comprehension are **weak/null** (EFL print [[sources/ej1079769]]; online gaze [[sources/beymer-font-size-type-online-reading]]).
- **Serif vs. sans** differences are minor once confounds controlled [[sources/richardson-legibility-serif-sans-serif]] · [[sources/lund-1999-knowledge-construction-typography]] — see [[concepts/serif-vs-sans]].
- **Disfluent** fonts (Sans Forgetica etc.) do not reliably help memory [[sources/dykes-sans-forgetica-readability]] · [[sources/ssrn-5678368]] — see [[concepts/disfluency]].

**Pattern.** Beyond gross legibility, *which* typeface you pick rarely changes comprehension; strong claims (serif superiority, disfluency benefits) are poorly supported.
