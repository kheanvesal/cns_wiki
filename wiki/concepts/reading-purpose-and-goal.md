# Reading Purpose & Goal

**What it is.** The reader's intention/task (study vs. entertainment; comprehend vs. skim vs. proofread vs. search) — the master moderator that determines which style/layout effects matter. **The interaction of goal × style is the organizing insight of this whole wiki.**

**Evidence.**
- Purpose changes online inferential behavior (and which inferences are generated), though not always offline scores [[sources/journal-of-educational-psychology-1999]].
- Comprehension is a **situated, goal-driven decision** [[sources/britt-et-al-dp-2022-r1]]; strategy vs. skill must be read jointly [[sources/kirby-style-strategy-skill-reading]].
- Search tasks induce selective, task-relevance-driven processing that reshapes comprehension [[sources/rouet-2002-search-tasks-comprehension]].
- Reading goal/task produces **global adjustments** to eye movements [[sources/reading-goal-task-eye-fixation-patterns]]; skim vs. comprehend changes gaze and outcomes [[sources/soltan-2026-skimming-clinical-info-eyetracking]] · [[sources/fitzsimmons-2014-skim-reading-web]].

**Implication.** Never record a style effect without its task context (CLAUDE.md prime rule). The same variable (font size, spacing, disfluency) helps one task and hurts another.

**Taxonomy of goals.** The wiki's four goals are reviewed in [[concepts/reading-goals-critique]] and reframed as a [[syntheses/reading-goals-purpose-depth-map]] (purpose × depth), which marks the uncovered goals (pleasure, learning-for-retention, careful evaluative reading).
