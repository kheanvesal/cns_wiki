# Skimming Strategy

**What it is.** Fast, selective reading for **gist/main points** rather than full detail. Distinct from **scanning** (locating a specific item, see [[concepts/scanning]]).

**How it works.**
- **Satisficing/foraging:** allocate attention to important/early text; stop at "good enough" [[sources/duggan-payne-2009-text-skimming]] · [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]] — see [[concepts/satisficing-foraging]].
- **Adaptive to task**; preserves gist, sacrifices detail [[sources/fitzsimmons-2014-skim-reading-web]] · [[sources/soltan-2026-skimming-clinical-info-eyetracking]].
- **Speed–accuracy tradeoff**, uneven across question types (main idea survives) [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2000-speed-patterns-screen]] — see [[concepts/skim-comprehension-tradeoff]].
- Supported by **headings/signaling** [[sources/lemarie-lorch-perywoodley-2012-headings]] and reader strategy differences [[sources/hyona-lorch-kaakinen-2002-reading-to-summarize]].
