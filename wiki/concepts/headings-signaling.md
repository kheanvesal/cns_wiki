# Headings & Signaling Devices

**What it is.** Typographic/positional cues (headings, whitespace, boldface, numbering) that signal text organization without adding content — help readers build macrostructure and navigate/skim.

**Evidence.**
- Headings carry **content** (topic) and **functional/structural** information; the **SARA** framework formalizes this [[sources/lemarie-lorch-perywoodley-2012-headings]].
- **Functional** (non-content) headings still direct selective attention and demarcate text objects [[sources/functional-headings-selective-attention]].
- Signaling supports skimming/summarizing by marking high-value regions [[sources/hyona-lorch-kaakinen-2002-reading-to-summarize]]; principle echoed in multimedia signaling [[sources/mayer-fiorella-intro-multimedia-learning]].

**Interaction.** Signaling helps most for **skimming/selective** reading and expository (structurally variable) text; benefit depends on reader goal and heading–structure alignment.
