# Characters-Per-Line (cpl)

**What it is.** The number of characters on a line — the variable that actually drives "line length" effects (physical width per se matters less). Main discussion lives in [[concepts/line-length]].

**Key points.**
- Dyson identifies **cpl** as the critical variable underlying line-length effects [[sources/layout-on-screen]]; ~55 cpl optimal for comprehension [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2001-speed-linelength-screen]].
- Craft norm ~45–75 (≈66) characters [[sources/bringhurst-elements-of-typographic-style]].
