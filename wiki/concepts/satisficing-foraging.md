# Satisficing / Information Foraging

**What it is.** The mechanism behind effective skimming: under time pressure readers **forage** for high-value content and read to a **"good enough" (satisficing)** threshold rather than reading everything.

**Evidence.**
- Readers allocate reading time to important content and extract main points effectively under time pressure [[sources/duggan-payne-2009-text-skimming]].
- Eye-tracking confirms selective fixation on informative regions with a satisficing stopping rule [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]].

**Why it matters.** Explains why skimming preserves gist far better than random text-sampling would, and why signaling/headings (which mark high-value regions) aid skimming.
