# Skim–Comprehension (Speed–Accuracy) Tradeoff

**What it is.** Reading faster/skimming lowers comprehension — but **not uniformly**: main-idea/gist information is retained far better than detail.

**Evidence.**
- Faster reading lowers comprehension, moderated by **question type** [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2000-speed-patterns-screen]] · [[sources/dyson-haselgrove-2001-speed-linelength-screen]].
- Skimming < careful reading on comprehension, but gist survives [[sources/fitzsimmons-2014-skim-reading-web]] · [[sources/soltan-2026-skimming-clinical-info-eyetracking]].
- Skimming is effective *because* readers satisfice toward main points [[sources/duggan-payne-2009-text-skimming]].

**Nuance.** The tradeoff is manageable, not catastrophic — the cost falls mostly on detail. Time pressure and medium (screen) worsen it [[sources/delgado-salmeron-2021-inattentive-onscreen-reading]].
