# Eye-Tracking Measures

**What it is.** Gaze-based indices of reading: **fixations** (count, duration), **saccades** (length, programming), **regressions**, and scanpaths. A distinct method type that can diverge from behavioral/self-report outcomes (see [[concepts/method-type-divergence]]).

**Used in the corpus by.** [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]] · [[sources/fitzsimmons-2014-skim-reading-web]] · [[sources/soltan-2026-skimming-clinical-info-eyetracking]] · [[sources/beymer-font-size-type-online-reading]] · [[sources/hyona-lorch-kaakinen-2002-reading-to-summarize]] · [[sources/huang-2017-line-spacing-simplified-chinese]] · [[sources/tarling-2009-page-layout-visual-search]] · [[sources/liang-2013-infographics-layout-search-eyetracking]] · [[sources/zuo-2023-target-layout-graphic-search-eyetracking]] · [[sources/3320435-3320447]] · [[sources/reading-goal-task-eye-fixation-patterns]] · [[sources/binkley-identifier-style-effort-comprehension-2012]].

**What it adds.** Reveals *process* (where/when attention goes) that outcome measures miss — e.g., font size shifts fixations without shifting comprehension; skimming shows selective fixation.

**Caveat.** Gaze effects don't always translate to comprehension/performance; report the linkage explicitly.
