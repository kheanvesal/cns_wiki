# Scanning

**What it is.** Reading to locate a **specific** target (a word, number, name) — distinct from **skimming** (reading for gist, [[concepts/skimming-strategy]]). Aligns with the [[syntheses/Search]] task.

**Key patterns.**
- Taught as a distinct strategy alongside skimming [[sources/tamsi-skimming-scanning-teaching-narrative]] · [[sources/reading-in-a-second-language-book]].
- Layout density/topology affects scanning speed [[sources/tarling-2009-page-layout-visual-search]] · [[sources/liang-2013-infographics-layout-search-eyetracking]]; scanning is faster than reading and less dependent on word-form cues [[sources/fisher-1975-reading-visual-search]].
