# Desirable Difficulty

**What it is.** The learning-theory claim that introducing effortful difficulty (including perceptual/typographic difficulty) can deepen encoding and improve later retention. The theoretical rationale behind [[concepts/disfluency]].

**Evidence — contested.**
- Pro: disfluent fonts improved retention [[sources/diemand-yauman-oppenheimer-fortune-favors-bold]].
- Con/null: Sans Forgetica and others fail to reproduce the benefit [[sources/dykes-sans-forgetica-readability]] · [[sources/font-disfluency-online-learning-highschool]] · [[sources/ssrn-5678368]].

**Nuance.** "Desirable difficulty" is well-supported for *other* manipulations (spacing of practice, testing) but **not** reliably for typographic disfluency. Keep the construct separate from its (weak) typographic instance.
