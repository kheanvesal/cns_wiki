# What Impacts Success in Proofreading? A Literature Review of Proofreading on Screen vs on Paper

**Citation.** Vitello, S. (2022). *What impacts success in proofreading? A literature review of proofreading on screen vs on paper.* Cambridge University Press & Assessment, Research Report (16 Nov 2022).

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (review; medium comparison).

**Question/aim.** Whether and why proofreading success differs between screen and paper.

**Method.** Narrative literature review.

**Style/layout variables (reviewed).** Presentation **medium** (screen vs. paper) and associated layout/interaction differences (scrolling, resolution, page format).

**Key findings.**
- Medium can affect proofreading/error-detection outcomes; screen reading often incurs costs vs. paper, but effects are moderated by task and text conditions.
- Complements the text-feature review with the medium dimension.

**Effect direction & size.** Review; mixed evidence, no single size.

**Limitations/caveats.** Grey literature; narrative synthesis; "screen vs paper" outcomes are notoriously moderated.
