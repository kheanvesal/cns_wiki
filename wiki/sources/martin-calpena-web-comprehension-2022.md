# Impact of the Layout on Web Comprehension

**Citation.** Yoldi Martín-Calpena, B., Iglesias Maqueda, A. M., & Morato Lara, J. L. (2022). Impact of the Layout on Web Comprehension. *ICCHP-AAATE 2022 Open Access Compendium, Part I*, 193–. doi:10.35011/icchp-aaate22-p1-25. Universidad Carlos III Madrid.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (web readability/accessibility).

**Question/aim.** Which web layout/structure elements relate to content comprehensibility, especially for users with disabilities, elderly, or low education.

**Method.** Corpus of varied web pages; extracted structural web elements (links, bold/italic, words per paragraph, etc.); trained ML classifier to rate pages by comprehension difficulty.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Web-page structural elements: links, bold/italic emphasis, paragraph length, page structure (observational, not experimental manipulation).

**Key findings.**
- Structural/layout web elements are predictive of comprehension difficulty and can classify pages by difficulty.
- Readability depends not only on language but on layout and content structuring.

**Effect direction & size.** ML classification model (accuracy per paper); no experimental effect sizes.

**Limitations/caveats.** Correlational/ML, not causal; comprehension "difficulty" is model-inferred; accessibility framing.
