# A Student Guide to Proofreading and Writing in Science

**Citation.** Hyatt, J.-P. K., Bienenstock, E. J., & Tilan, J. U. (2017). A student guide to proofreading and writing in science. *Advances in Physiology Education*, 41, 324–331. doi:10.1152/advan.00004.2017.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (pedagogical "personal view").

**Question/aim.** Provide undergraduates strategies for proofreading and improving scientific writing readability.

**Method.** Practitioner/pedagogical article; catalogue of common student writing mistakes + correction strategies (word choice, structure, approach).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None experimental — targets writing/proofreading *strategy* and readability at the composition level.

**Key findings.**
- Proofreading is an iterative process needing practice and feedback; rarely taught explicitly.
- Recommends concrete word-choice, structure, and audience-focus strategies to enhance readability of drafts.

**Effect direction & size.** N/A (guidance).

**Limitations/caveats.** Opinion/guide, not empirical; discipline-specific (science writing).
