# Reading and Visual Search

**Citation.** Fisher, D. F. (1975). Reading and visual search. *Memory & Cognition*, 3(2), 188–196.

**Reading task(s).** Search vs. reading (direct comparison).

**Question/aim.** How word shape and word boundary manipulations affect reading vs. visual search.

**Method.** Four experiments. Word shape = type (normal / all-caps / aLtErNaTiNg case); word boundary = spacing (normal / filled / absent), combined factorially (9 space-type conditions). Exp. III–IV added form-class expectancy and target predictability in high-speed paragraph search.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Letter case / word shape; inter-word spacing (word boundary).

**Key findings.**
- Search is 2–2.5× faster than reading.
- Removing spaces + altering type slows **both** reading and search to ~1/3 normal speed.
- Type × Space interaction significant for **reading but not search** — reading relies more on word-shape/boundary cues.
- Expected/predictable target words found faster (top-down guidance).

**Effect direction & size.** Large speed reductions (~66%) from degrading word boundary/shape; search less affected by the interaction than reading.

**Limitations/caveats.** 1975 print; small classic-era samples; tachistoscopic-era methods.
