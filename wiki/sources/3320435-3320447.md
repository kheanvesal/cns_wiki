# Impact of English Reading Comprehension Abilities on Processing Magazine-Style Narrative Visualizations (MSNV)

**Citation.** Toker, D., Moro, R., Simko, J., Bielikova, M., & Conati, C. (2019). Impact of English Reading Comprehension Abilities on Processing Magazine Style Narrative Visualizations and Implications for Personalization. *ACM UMAP '19*.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (text + embedded data visualizations).

**Question/aim.** How reading-comprehension ability shapes how users process documents with embedded visualizations (MSNVs), toward real-time personalized support.

**Method.** Eye-tracking (gaze) + performance analysis across two user studies (Canada; a non-English-speaking European country).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Text–graphic integration (MSNV layout), references between text and visualization; individual difference = reading-comprehension ability.

**Key findings.**
- Reading-comprehension ability significantly modulates gaze behavior and processing of text–visualization material.
- Split-attention risk: repeated transitions between text and graphics raise cognitive load; lower-ability readers are more affected.
- Supports personalizing attention-guidance/cuing to reader ability.

**Effect direction & size.** Ability significantly predicts MSNV processing; specific sizes per-study (not summarized here).

**Limitations/caveats.** Lab eye-tracking; specialized MSNV materials; personalization implications are prospective.
