# Developing Optimum Interface Design for On-Screen Chinese Proofreading Tasks

**Citation.** Chan, A. H. S., So, J. C. Y., & Tsang, S. N. H. (2011). Developing Optimum Interface Design for On-Screen Chinese Proofreading Tasks. *Springer LNCS* (978-3-642-21669-5_1). City University of Hong Kong.

> **Duplicate note.** Identical file also present as `Developing_Optimum_Interface_Design_for.pdf`. Ingested once.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (screen, Chinese).

**Question/aim.** Review display factors affecting on-screen Chinese proofreading and introduce a Chinese Proofreading System to identify optimum interface settings.

**Method.** Review of empirical display-factor studies + description of a proofreading experiment system; distinguishes **comparison** vs. **noncomparison** proofreading.

**Style/layout variables manipulated (reviewed/targeted).** Typeface, font size, number of text lines, text direction, copy placement — vs. speed, accuracy, and subjective preference.

**Key findings.**
- Frames comparison proofreading (dead copy vs. live copy) as the critical error-detection process.
- Multiple display factors jointly shape proofreading speed/accuracy/preference; aims to derive design recommendations.

**Effect direction & size.** Review/system paper; no single effect size.

**Limitations/caveats.** Partly programmatic (proposes system + research ideas); Chinese-specific.
