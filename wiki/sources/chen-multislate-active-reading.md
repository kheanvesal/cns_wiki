# Designing a Multi-Slate Reading Environment to Support Active Reading Activities

**Citation.** Chen, N., Guimbretière, F., & Sellen, A. (2012). Designing a Multi-Slate Reading Environment to Support Active Reading Activities. *ACM Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction (TOCHI)*.

**Reading task(s).** Active reading — spans skimming, cross-document search, and comprehension.

**Question/aim.** Can an interconnected set of thin electronic slates replicate paper's advantages for active reading (annotating, cross-referencing, navigating multiple documents)?

**Method.** System design + construction; preliminary user study; grounded in the paper-vs-digital active-reading literature.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Display configuration — multiple linked slates vs. single screen; spatial layout of documents across devices.

**Key findings.**
- Paper's dominance stems from supporting diverse **active-reading** needs (quick navigation, spatial layout, annotation).
- A multi-slate environment can approximate paper's flexibility, combining advantages of e-books, PCs, and tabletops.
- Spatial/multi-display layout supports skimming across and searching within documents.

**Effect direction & size.** Design study; preliminary usability evidence (no controlled effect size).

**Limitations/caveats.** Prototype + preliminary study; not a controlled reading-performance experiment.
