# GenAI-Powered Text Personalization: NLP Validation of Adaptation Capabilities

**Citation.** McNamara, D. S., et al. (2025). GenAI-Powered Text Personalization: Natural Language Processing Validation of Adaptation Capabilities. *Applied Sciences*, 15(12), 6791.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (adaptive/personalized text).

**Question/aim.** Can LLMs tailor texts to reader profiles (knowledge, reading skill, goals), and can NLP validate the adaptation?

**Method.** Two experiments. **Exp 1:** four LLMs (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Llama, Gemini Pro 1.5, ChatGPT-4) adapt 10 science texts (bio/chem/physics) to 4 reader profiles; NLP evaluates linguistic/semantic readability features. **Exp 2:** prompts augmented with personification, chain-of-thought, and RAG.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** **Linguistic** — LLM-driven adaptation of readability features, esp. **cohesion** (L3) and lexical/semantic complexity (L1); reader profile as target. (Directly addresses the corpus's under-studied linguistic axes.)

**Key findings.**
- Baseline LLM prompts showed **inconsistent alignment** between reader ability and text complexity.
- **Augmented prompts** (personification / chain-of-thought / RAG) produced **better cohesion alignment** for high- vs low-knowledge readers.
- NLP offers real-time, theory-driven validation of personalization.

**Effect direction & size.** Augmented > baseline prompts for profile alignment (NLP-measured; no reader-outcome experiment).

**Limitations/caveats.** Validation is **NLP-metric**, not behavioral comprehension; hypothetical profiles.
