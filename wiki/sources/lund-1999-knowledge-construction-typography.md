# Knowledge Construction in Typography: The Case of Legibility Research and the Legibility of Sans Serif Typefaces

**Citation.** Lund, O. (1999). *Knowledge construction in typography: the case of legibility research and the legibility of sans serif typefaces.* PhD thesis, Department of Typography & Graphic Communication, University of Reading.

**Reading task(s).** Legibility (meta/methodological critique).

**Question/aim.** A critical, meta-scientific examination of how legibility knowledge (esp. serif vs. sans) has been constructed and whether its claims are warranted.

**Method.** Historical + methodological analysis of the legibility-research literature.

**Style/layout variables (examined).** Serif vs. sans serif legibility claims; the methods used to establish them.

**Key findings.**
- Much received wisdom on typeface legibility rests on **weak or confounded methodology**.
- Claims that serif aids legibility (or sans is better on screen) are often **not well-supported** by the cited evidence.
- Calls for methodological rigor and caution in generalizing legibility findings.

**Effect direction & size.** Meta-critique — argues effects are overstated/underdetermined.

**Limitations/caveats.** 1999 thesis; critique rather than new experiment.
