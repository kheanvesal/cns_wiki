# Got It! Prompting Readability Using ChatGPT to Enhance Academic Texts for Diverse Learning Needs

**Citation.** Hedlin, E., Estling, L., Wong, J., Demmans Epp, C., & Viberg, O. (2025). Got It! Prompting Readability Using ChatGPT to Enhance Academic Texts for Diverse Learning Needs. *ACM (LAK '25 / Learning Analytics & Knowledge)*, doi:10.1145/3706468.3706483. (KTH / Utrecht / Univ. Alberta.)

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension / accessibility (academic-text simplification).

**Question/aim.** Can ChatGPT-4 simplify academic texts, and which **prompting strategies** work best — especially for students with reading difficulties (e.g., dyslexia)?

**Method.** ChatGPT-4 re-wrote **136 academic texts** under **four prompting approaches**: Standard, Meta, Roleplay, Chain-of-Thought. Readability evaluated (intrinsic metrics).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Content-level — LLM **text simplification** (L1 lexical / L2 syntactic / L3 cohesion) driven by prompt strategy.

**Key findings.**
- **All four prompts improved readability**; **Meta** performed best overall.
- The **Standard** prompt sometimes produced texts **less readable than the original** — prompt design matters.
- High variability across simplified texts → match strategy to individual learner needs.

**Effect direction & size.** Meta > others; Standard occasionally worse than source (intrinsic readability).

**Limitations/caveats.** **Intrinsic** readability metrics, not a reader-outcome experiment; academic-text genre.
