# The Implementation of Skimming and Scanning Strategies in Teaching Reading Narrative Text to Tenth-Grade Students

**Citation.** Tamsi, R. K., Zuhri, F., & Kurniasih, E. The Implementation of Skimming and Scanning Strategies in Teaching Reading Narrative Text to the Tenth Grade Students of SMAN 21 Surabaya. English Department, State University of Surabaya.

**Reading task(s).** Skimming + scanning (pedagogical).

**Question/aim.** How skimming and scanning strategies are implemented (and received) when teaching narrative-text reading to secondary EFL students.

**Method.** Classroom implementation study (qualitative/descriptive) of teaching skimming (gist) and scanning (specific info) strategies.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None — instructional **strategy** (skimming vs. scanning as taught techniques).

**Key findings.**
- Distinguishes **skimming** (reading for gist/main idea) from **scanning** (locating specific information) as teachable strategies.
- Reports implementation of these strategies improves student engagement/reading of narrative text.

**Effect direction & size.** Descriptive/pedagogical; no controlled effect size.

**Limitations/caveats.** Single classroom; descriptive; EFL secondary context. Useful mainly for the **skim vs. scan terminology**.
