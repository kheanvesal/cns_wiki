# The Effects of Reading Speed and Reading Patterns on the Understanding of Text Read from Screen

**Citation.** Dyson, M. C., & Haselgrove, M. (2000). The effects of reading speed and reading patterns on the understanding of text read from screen. *Journal of Research in Reading*, 23(2), 210–223.

**Reading task(s).** Skimming/fast reading vs. normal (screen).

**Question/aim.** How reading speed (normal vs. fast/skim) and scrolling patterns affect comprehension of screen text.

**Method.** Experiment; comprehension measured with multiple question types at normal vs. fast reading speed; automatic recording of scrolling to infer reading patterns.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Reading speed; reading/scrolling pattern.

**Key findings.**
- **Speed–accuracy tradeoff**: fast reading lowers comprehension, but not uniformly across question types (main-idea items survive better than detail).
- Distinct **reading patterns** (scrolling behavior) relate to comprehension outcomes.

**Effect direction & size.** Faster reading → lower comprehension, moderated by question type.

**Limitations/caveats.** Early screen tech; scrolling-based pattern inference.
