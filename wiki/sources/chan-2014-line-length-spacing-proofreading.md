# Effects of Line Length, Line Spacing, and Line Number on Proofreading Performance and Scrolling of Chinese Text

**Citation.** Chan, A. H. S., Tsang, S. N. H., & Ng, A. W. Y. (2014). Effects of Line Length, Line Spacing, and Line Number on Proofreading Performance and Scrolling of Chinese Text. *Human Factors* (pub. online 2013; doi:10.1177/0018720813499368). City University of Hong Kong.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (screen, Chinese).

**Question/aim.** Effects and interactions of line length, line number, and line spacing on Chinese on-screen proofreading performance and amount of scrolling.

**Method.** Controlled experiment manipulating 3 display factors; measured proofreading time, error-detection rate, and scrolling amount; correlated performance with scrolling.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Line length, line number, line spacing (screen size–related).

**Key findings.**
- Line number and line spacing had significant main **and interaction** effects on both proofreading time and detection rate.
- Line length and line number significantly influenced scrolling amount.
- Layout factors trade off performance against scrolling effort.

**Effect direction & size.** Significant main + interaction effects (see paper for magnitudes).

**Limitations/caveats.** Chinese text specifics may not transfer to alphabetic scripts; lab task.
