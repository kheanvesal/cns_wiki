# Supporting Students' Reading and Cognition with AI

**Citation.** (2025). *Supporting Students' Reading and Cognition with AI.* arXiv:2504.13900. (Author list per arXiv.)

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (AI-supported academic reading).

**Question/aim.** How do students' prompts to AI tools shape their reading process and cognitive engagement over time?

**Method.** Analysis of text from **124 sessions** of students using AI tools while doing assigned course readings; prompts categorized by **Bloom's Taxonomy** (Remembering → Understanding → Applying → Analyzing → Evaluating).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None — observational analysis of AI-reading interaction patterns.

**Key findings.**
- **Within a session**, higher-order prompts ("Analyzing", "Evaluating") become more prevalent in the 2nd/3rd prompts — a shift toward deeper thinking.
- **Across weeks**, users **converge toward passive reading engagement** — a longitudinal shallowing.
- Design implications: scaffold low-level tasks, proactively prompt higher-order thinking, add adaptive human-in-the-loop control.

**Effect direction & size.** Descriptive/qualitative (prompt taxonomy + temporal trend).

**Limitations/caveats.** Preprint; observational; single course; no control group.
