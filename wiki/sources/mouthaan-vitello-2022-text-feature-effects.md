# What Impacts Success in Proofreading? A Literature Review of Text Feature Effects

**Citation.** Mouthaan, M., & Vitello, S. (2022). *What impacts success in proofreading? A literature review of text feature effects.* Cambridge University Press & Assessment, Research Report (16 Nov 2022).

> **Duplicate note.** Two identical copies in folder (`...text-feature-effects.` and `... (1)`). Ingested once.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (review).

**Question/aim.** Synthesize how text features affect proofreading success (error detection).

**Method.** Narrative literature review.

**Style/layout variables manipulated (reviewed).** Text features spanning typography, layout, and linguistic properties that influence how easily errors are spotted.

**Key findings.**
- Proofreading success depends on text features that affect how errors "pop out" vs. get missed.
- Reviews evidence on how presentation features can help or hinder error detection (complements the on-screen-vs-paper review [[sources/vitello-2022-screen-vs-paper]]).

**Effect direction & size.** Review; no single effect size.

**Limitations/caveats.** Grey literature (assessment-body report); narrative not meta-analytic.
