# Understanding How Headings Influence Text Processing

**Citation.** Lemarié, J., Lorch, R. F., Jr., & Péry-Woodley, M.-P. (2012). Understanding How Headings Influence Text Processing. *Discours*, 10. doi:10.4000/discours.8600.

> **Duplicate note.** Stored 3× (`discours-8600`, `discours-8600 (1)`, `Understanding_How_Headings_Influence_Text_Processi`). Ingested once.

**Reading task(s).** Skimming/selective reading + comprehension.

**Question/aim.** How headings — as **signaling devices** — influence how readers process and navigate text.

**Method.** Theoretical/review synthesis; develops the **SARA** framework of signaling devices (headings carry content + functional/structural information).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Headings (typographic + positional signaling), whitespace/demarcation.

**Key findings.**
- Headings serve dual roles: convey **content** (topic) and **functional** information (structure/demarcation).
- They guide selective attention, aid navigation, and support building a text macrostructure.
- Signaling effects depend on reader goal and how headings map to text organization.
