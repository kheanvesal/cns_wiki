# Improving Reading Comprehension of Expository Texts through Text-Structure-Aligned Graphic Organizers

**Citation.** Marginingsih, Muhtarom, M., & Sundari (2025). Improving Reading Comprehension of Expository Texts through Text-Structure-Aligned Graphic Organizers for Higher Education Students. *Educational Process: International Journal*, 18, e2025502.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension + summarization (ESP, expository).

**Question/aim.** Impact of matching graphic-organizer type to text structure on comprehension and written summarization.

**Method.** Explanatory sequential mixed-methods; N=200 Indonesian university students (Information Systems/Informatics). T-Charts (cause–effect) vs. Venn Diagrams (compare–contrast) + summary writing scored on a 4-dimension rubric; open-ended perception questionnaires.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Graphic-organizer format aligned (or not) to the text's rhetorical structure.

**Key findings.**
- Comprehension significantly higher with T-Charts (M=3.67) vs. Venn Diagrams (M=3.24), medium effect (Cohen's d = 0.56).
- Structure-aligned organizers act as a cognitive/strategic scaffold for expository reading and summarization.

**Effect direction & size.** T-Chart > Venn, d = 0.56 (medium).

**Limitations/caveats.** Comparison confounds organizer type with text type (cause-effect vs. compare-contrast); self-report perceptions; single institution.
