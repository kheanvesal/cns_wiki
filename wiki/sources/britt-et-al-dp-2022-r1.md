# Reading Contexts, Goals, and Decisions: Text Comprehension as a Situated Activity

**Citation.** Britt, M. A., Durik, A., & Rouet, J.-F. (2022). Reading Contexts, Goals, and Decisions: Text Comprehension as a Situated Activity. *Discourse Processes*, 59(5–6), 361–378. doi:10.1080/0163853X.2022.2068345.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (theoretical framework spanning study, search, skimming).

**Question/aim.** Theoretical account of how reading context and reader goals shape comprehension as a *situated* decision-making activity (extends the RESOLV / task-model line).

**Method.** Conceptual/theoretical review (no new experiment).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None directly; frames how task context governs the *depth* and *strategy* of reading, which downstream interacts with style/layout effects.

**Key findings.**
- Comprehension depth is a function of the reader's goal and the task context, not just the text.
- Readers form a task model and make ongoing decisions about how much to read and how deeply.
- Provides the goal/context scaffolding that explains why the same style variable helps one task and hurts another.

**Effect direction & size.** N/A (theory).

**Limitations/caveats.** Framework, not empirical test; predictions need operationalization.
