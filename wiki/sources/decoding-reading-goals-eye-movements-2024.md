# Decoding Reading Goals from Eye Movements

**Citation.** (2024). *Decoding Reading Goals from Eye Movements.* arXiv:2410.20779. (Author list per arXiv.)

**Reading task(s).** Search (information seeking) vs Comprehension — decoding the reader's goal.

**Question/aim.** Can a reader's **goal** — information seeking vs ordinary reading for comprehension — be decoded from their eye movements?

**Method.** Large-scale eye-tracking data; many models spanning architectures/representations, plus a new **model ensemble**; mixed-effects analysis of what makes items/participants hard to classify. Transformer models with **scanpath + language-modeling** representations perform best.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — decodes the reading **goal** from gaze (LLM-era method applied to the reading-goal question).

**Key findings.**
- Reading goal (seek vs comprehend) is **decodable from eye movements**, accurately and **in real time** — long before the reader finishes.
- Textual and participant properties modulate difficulty.
- Empirical support for treating information-seeking and comprehension as distinguishable **regimes**.

**Effect direction & size.** Above-chance real-time classification (see paper for accuracy).

**Limitations/caveats.** Preprint; binary goal contrast (only two regimes); dataset-specific.
