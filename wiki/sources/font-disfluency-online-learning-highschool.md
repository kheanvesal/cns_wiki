# The Effects of Font Disfluency on Information Retention and Reading Speed of High School Students in Online Learning

**Citation.** *The Effects of Font Disfluency on Information Retention and Reading Speed of High School Students in Online Learning.* Student research paper, Academic Magnet High School (word count ~4,764). (Author/venue as in file `EffectsOfFontDisfluency.pdf`.)

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension/retention + reading speed (online learning).

**Question/aim.** Whether font disfluency affects information retention and reading speed for high-school students online.

**Method.** Experimental comparison of fonts — Times New Roman (fluent), Monotype Corsiva, and others — on retention and reading speed.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Font disfluency (fluent vs. disfluent typefaces).

**Key findings.**
- Font choice affects reading speed (disfluent fonts read slower).
- Retention benefits of disfluency were not robustly supported — consistent with the failed-replication side of the debate.

**Effect direction & size.** Disfluent fonts slow reading; retention gains weak/absent.

**Limitations/caveats.** Student project; single school sample; limited power.
