# Individual Differences in Reading to Summarize Expository Text: Evidence From Eye Fixation Patterns

**Citation.** Hyönä, J., Lorch, R. F., Jr., & Kaakinen, J. K. (2002). Individual Differences in Reading to Summarize Expository Text: Evidence From Eye Fixation Patterns. *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 94(1), 44–55.

> **Duplicate note.** Stored 2× (`...exp` and `...exp (1)`). Ingested once.

**Reading task(s).** Reading to summarize (selective reading; skimming-adjacent) + comprehension.

**Question/aim.** Identify adult reader strategies when reading multi-topic expository text to summarize, via eye fixations.

**Method.** Eye-tracking; adults read multiple-topic expository texts to summarize; fixation-pattern clustering into strategy types.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — text topic structure; individual reader strategy is the focus.

**Key findings.**
- Distinct reader types: e.g., **fast linear** readers, **slow linear** readers, and **topic-structure processors** who reread at topic shifts.
- Strategy relates to how readers process headings/topic boundaries and to summary quality.
- Shows that "reading to summarize" recruits selective, structure-sensitive processing.

**Effect direction & size.** Strategy typology (qualitative clusters) with fixation-metric differences.

**Limitations/caveats.** Eye-tracking sample; Finnish/expository materials.
