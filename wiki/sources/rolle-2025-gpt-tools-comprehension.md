# Differential Effects of GPT-Based Tools on Comprehension of Standardized Passages

**Citation.** Rolle, C. E., et al. (2025). Differential effects of GPT-based tools on comprehension of standardized passages. *Frontiers in Education*, 10, 1506752. (Stanford Univ.)

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (AI-assisted).

**Question/aim.** How four GPT-based tools affect reading comprehension, and whether the effect depends on the reader's baseline ability.

**Method.** Pre-registered **randomized cross-over online study, n=195**; ACT-derived standardized passages. Four tools: AI-generated **summaries**, AI-generated **outlines**, a **Q&A tutor chatbot**, and a **Socratic discussion chatbot**.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — AI tool type; individual difference = baseline reading-comprehension ability.

**Key findings.**
- **Ability-dependent (aptitude × treatment interaction):** AI tools **improved** comprehension for **lower** performers and **worsened** it for **higher** performers.
- By tool: low performers benefited most from the **Socratic chatbot**; high performers were harmed most by the **summary** tool.
- Blanket deployment can harm strong readers.

**Effect direction & size.** Significant tool × ability interaction (see paper).

**Limitations/caveats.** Online; standardized short passages; single session.
