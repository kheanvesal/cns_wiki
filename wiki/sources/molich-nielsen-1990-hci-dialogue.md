# Improving a Human-Computer Dialogue

**Citation.** Molich, R., & Nielsen, J. (1990). Improving a Human-Computer Dialogue. *Communications of the ACM*, 33(3), 338–348.

**Reading task(s).** Peripheral — general UI usability (filed under Skimming, but not a reading-style study).

**Question/aim.** How to identify and reduce usability problems in human-computer dialogue design (origin of the usability **heuristics** later formalized as Nielsen's 10 heuristics).

**Method.** Survey of 77 designers/programmers evaluating an interface; heuristic-evaluation methodology.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None reading-specific — interface dialogue design principles.

**Key findings.**
- Identifying specific usability problems is hard even for motivated professionals.
- Introduces a small set of usability heuristics for interface evaluation.

**Effect direction & size.** N/A (methodology paper).
