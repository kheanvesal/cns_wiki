# Online Health Information Seeking Behaviour: Understanding Different Search Approaches

**Citation.** Pang, P. C.-I., Chang, S., Pearce, J., & Verspoor, K. (2014). Online Health Information Seeking Behaviour: Understanding Different Search Approaches. *PACIS 2014 Proceedings*. University of Melbourne.

**Reading task(s).** Search (health-information domain).

**Question/aim.** Characterize the different approaches people take when seeking health information online.

**Method.** Empirical study of online health-information-seeking behaviour; qualitative/behavioral analysis of search approaches.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — domain study of **search approaches/strategies** (context for how layout/structure could support health seekers).

**Key findings.**
- Users adopt distinct approaches to online health search (varying in directedness, source use, and query behavior).
- Design implications for presenting/structuring health content to match seeker approaches.

**Effect direction & size.** Qualitative typology; no standardized effect size.

**Limitations/caveats.** Domain-specific (health); design implications indirect for typography/layout.
