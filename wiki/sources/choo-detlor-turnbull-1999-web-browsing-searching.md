# Information Seeking on the Web — An Integrated Model of Browsing and Searching

**Citation.** Choo, C. W., Detlor, B., & Turnbull, D. (1999). Information Seeking on the Web—An Integrated Model of Browsing and Searching. Paper, 62nd ASIS Annual Meeting. (ERIC ED438801.)

**Reading task(s).** Search + browsing/scanning.

**Question/aim.** An integrated model linking browsing and searching as modes of web information seeking.

**Method.** Field study of knowledge-workers' web use; behavioral modeling (combines Aguilar/Ellis scanning modes with search).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None directly — defines **behavioral modes** (undirected viewing, conditioned viewing, informal/formal search) that different layouts serve.

**Key findings.**
- Web information seeking spans a continuum from **browsing/scanning** to **directed search**.
- Users move fluidly among modes; effective sites/layouts must support both scanning and targeted lookup.

**Effect direction & size.** N/A (model).

**Limitations/caveats.** 1999 web; conceptual model from field data; predates modern search UIs.
