# A Synthesis of Reading Interventions and Effects on Reading Comprehension Outcomes for Older Struggling Readers

**Citation.** Edmonds, M. S., Vaughn, S., Wexler, J., Reutebuch, C., Cable, A., Tackett, K. K., & Schnakenberg, J. W. (2009). A Synthesis of Reading Interventions and Effects on Reading Comprehension Outcomes for Older Struggling Readers. *Review of Educational Research*, 79(1), 262–300.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (intervention synthesis).

**Question/aim.** What reading interventions improve comprehension for older (grades 6–12) struggling readers.

**Method.** Systematic research synthesis/meta-analysis of intervention studies.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None — instructional **interventions** (comprehension strategies, decoding, fluency, vocabulary), not text style.

**Key findings.**
- **Comprehension-strategy** and multi-component interventions produced meaningful gains in comprehension for older struggling readers.
- Effects on comprehension outcomes were substantial even when word-level gains were modest.

**Effect direction & size.** Positive comprehension effects (moderate; see paper for standardized mean differences).

**Limitations/caveats.** Intervention (not style) focus; adolescent struggling-reader population. Context page for who benefits from comprehension support.
