# Sans Forgetica: Font Readability Experimental Design

**Citation.** Dykes, R. *Sans Forgetica: Font Readability Experimental Design.* Research note, Faculty of Human Studies, Jin-ai University (bilingual EN/JP).

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension/memory (disfluency font).

**Question/aim.** Design/justification for studying Sans Forgetica's readability via eye-tracking + quantitative analysis, and why prior "desirable difficulty" effects fail to appear.

**Method.** Research-design note (proposes eye-tracking + quantitative readability analysis); reviews prior Sans Forgetica findings.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Sans Forgetica (a font engineered to be disfluent) vs. baselines.

**Key findings / argument.**
- Sans Forgetica was designed to create **desirable difficulty**, but prior research does **not** find the intended memory benefit.
- Proposes that Sans Forgetica may not actually be **disfluent** — prior disfluency judgments were implicit/unvalidated.
- Explicit measurement (eye-tracking) could reveal why the effect is absent.

**Effect direction & size.** Argues null / absent desirable-difficulty effect; proposes empirical test.

**Limitations/caveats.** Design/position note, not a completed experiment.
