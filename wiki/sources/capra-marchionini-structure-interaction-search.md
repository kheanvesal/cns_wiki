# Effects of Structure and Interaction Style on Distinct Search Tasks

**Citation.** Capra, R., Marchionini, G., Oh, J. S., Stutzman, F., & Zhang, Y. (2007). Effects of Structure and Interaction Style on Distinct Search Tasks. *JCDL '07*. UNC Chapel Hill.

> **Duplicate note.** Two identical copies (`1255175.1255267.pdf` and `(1)`). Ingested once.

**Reading task(s).** Search (digital libraries).

**Question/aim.** How information **structure** and **interaction style** interact with different search-task types (e.g., lookup vs. exploratory).

**Method.** User study comparing interaction styles/structures across distinct search tasks; performance + behavior.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Information structure/representation and interaction style of the search UI.

**Key findings.**
- Successful DL access depends on searcher characteristics, search-task goals, and the system — this work targets the task × system interplay.
- The best structure/interaction style **depends on the search-task type** (no single winner).

**Effect direction & size.** Task-dependent interaction effects (see paper).

**Limitations/caveats.** Digital-library UI context; interaction-style effects may be system-specific.
