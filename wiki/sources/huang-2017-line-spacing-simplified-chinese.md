# An Eye Tracking Study of Line Spacing Effects on Reading Simplified Chinese

**Citation.** Huang, D. (2017). *An eye tracking study of line spacing effects on reading Simplified Chinese.* Outstanding Academic Papers by Students (OAPS), City University of Hong Kong. http://dspace.cityu.edu.hk/handle/2031/99

**Reading task(s).** Reading/proofreading of Simplified Chinese (eye-tracking).

**Question/aim.** How line spacing affects eye-movement behavior when reading Simplified Chinese on screen.

**Method.** Eye-tracking experiment (fixations/saccades) across line-spacing conditions; student research paper.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Line spacing (leading).

**Key findings.**
- Line spacing modulates eye-movement patterns (fixation/saccade behavior) in Chinese reading.
- Adds eye-tracking evidence complementing the Chan group's behavioral line-spacing findings.

**Effect direction & size.** Eye-movement differences by spacing (see paper for detail).

**Limitations/caveats.** Student paper; Chinese-specific; modest sample typical of eye-tracking studies.
