# Scrolling and Pagination for Within-Document Searching: The Impact of Screen Size and Interaction Style

**Citation.** Hemminger, B., & Marcial, L. (2012). Scrolling and pagination for within document searching: The impact of screen size and interaction style. *Proceedings of the Association for Information Science and Technology*. UNC Chapel Hill.

**Reading task(s).** Within-document search + skimming.

**Question/aim.** How screen size and interaction style (scrolling vs. pagination) affect within-document information search.

**Method.** User study comparing scrolling vs. pagination across screen sizes; search-performance measures.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Interaction style (scrolling vs. paging); screen size.

**Key findings.**
- Scrolling vs. pagination affects within-document search efficiency, and the best choice **interacts with screen size**.
- Smaller screens change the tradeoffs between the two navigation styles.

**Effect direction & size.** Interaction-style × screen-size effects on search (see paper).

**Limitations/caveats.** 2012 devices; task/document specific.
