# Information-Seeking Behaviours and Cognitive Search Strategies in Different Search Tasks on the WWW

**Citation.** Thatcher, A. (2006). Information-seeking behaviours and cognitive search strategies in different search tasks on the WWW. *International Journal of Industrial Ergonomics*, 36, 1055–1068.

**Reading task(s).** Search (WWW).

**Question/aim.** Identify cognitive search strategies and how they differ by task type.

**Method.** N=80; two researcher-defined and two participant-defined tasks, each split into **directed search** vs. **general-purpose browsing**; retrospective verbal protocols + log files + observation; correspondence analysis. **12 cognitive search strategies** identified.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — strategy choice as a function of **task type**; implications for how content/navigation should be represented.

**Key findings.**
- Strategy choice differs between researcher- vs. participant-defined tasks and between directed vs. browsing tasks.
- Provides a taxonomy (12 strategies) useful for site/search-engine design.

**Effect direction & size.** Task-type differences via correspondence analysis (categorical).

**Limitations/caveats.** 2006 web; verbal-protocol reactivity; strategy taxonomy is descriptive.
