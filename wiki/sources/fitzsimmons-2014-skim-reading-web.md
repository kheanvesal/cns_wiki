# Skim Reading: An Adaptive Strategy for Reading on the Web

**Citation.** Fitzsimmons, G., Weal, M. J., & Drieghe, D. (2014). Skim Reading: An Adaptive Strategy for Reading on the Web. *WebSci '14*. University of Southampton.

**Reading task(s).** Skimming (hypertext/webpages).

**Question/aim.** How skim reading of hypertext/webpages differs from normal reading and what it costs comprehension.

**Method.** Eye-tracking; participants instructed to read for comprehension vs. skim; comprehension questions on the texts.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Reading instruction (skim vs. comprehend); hypertext format.

**Key findings.**
- Skimming is an **adaptive** strategy: readers reduce fixations/time while still capturing gist.
- Skimming reduces comprehension relative to careful reading, but readers modulate depth to task demands.
- Adds hypertext-specific evidence (prior work focused on linear text).

**Effect direction & size.** Skim < comprehension-read on comprehension; large reading-time reduction.

**Limitations/caveats.** Lab eye-tracking; specific hypertext materials.
