# The Effect of Emphasizing Proofreading and Selected Basic English Rules in a Beginning Typewriting Class at the College Level

**Citation.** Snyder, V. H. (1978). *The Effect of Emphasizing Proofreading and Selected Basic English Rules in a Beginning Typewriting Class at the College Level.* Doctoral dissertation (Ed.D.), The University of Alabama.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (instructional/pedagogical).

**Question/aim.** Whether explicitly emphasizing proofreading and basic English rules improves outcomes in a college beginning-typewriting course.

**Method.** Instructional intervention study (quasi-experimental classroom design, 1970s).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** None typographic — the manipulation is **instructional emphasis** on proofreading, not text presentation.

**Key findings.**
- Explicit proofreading instruction is the lever here (pedagogy), relevant as context for how proofreading skill develops rather than how layout affects it.

**Effect direction & size.** Historical dissertation; specific results not extracted.

**Limitations/caveats.** 1978, typewriting context; off-center for style/layout effects — retain as a pedagogy/skill-acquisition reference.
