# 小型手持裝置螢幕之版面留白對閱讀績效和美感喜好的影響 (Effect of Screen Whitespace on Reading Performance & Aesthetic Preference on Small Handheld Devices)

**Citation.** Huang & Li (黃室苗 & 李武鉦) (2017). *The Effects of White Space in Layout on Reading Performance and Aesthetic Preference on Small Handheld Device Screens.* MOST research report, grant 105-2221-E-150-034, National Formosa University (國立虎尾科技大學), Taiwan. (English title per `Readability Latent-Factor Matrix.xlsx`.) Central design: a 3×2×2 manipulation of page margins/white space.

**Reading task(s).** Reading performance + aesthetics on handheld screens (filed under proofreading).

**Question/aim.** How page whitespace/margins on small handheld screens affect reading performance and aesthetic preference.

**Method.** Experimental (Chinese-language government-funded research report); manipulated whitespace/margin layout; measured reading performance and aesthetic-preference ratings.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Whitespace / margins (版面留白) on small screens.

**Key findings.**
- Whitespace/margin layout affects both reading performance and aesthetic preference on small handheld screens.
- Performance-optimal and aesthetically-preferred whitespace levels can differ (performance-vs-preference tension).

**Effect direction & size.** See report (Chinese); specific magnitudes not extracted here.

**Limitations/caveats.** Chinese-language grant report; small-screen and script-specific; full text not translated during ingest.
