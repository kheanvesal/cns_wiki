# An Eye Tracking Study of How Font Size and Type Influence Online Reading

**Citation.** Beymer, D., Russell, D., & Orton, P. (2008). An Eye Tracking Study of How Font Size and Type Influence Online Reading. *BCS HCI / IBM Almaden Research Center*.

**Reading task(s).** Online reading (relevant to skimming and comprehension).

**Question/aim.** How font size and font type affect eye-movement behavior during online reading.

**Method.** Eye-tracking of on-screen reading across font sizes and types (serif vs. sans).

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Font size; font type (serif vs. sans serif).

**Key findings.**
- Font size affects eye-movement behavior (fixation duration/number) — larger sizes ease processing.
- Font type (serif vs. sans) shows weaker/inconsistent effects on reading behavior.
- Effects appear in gaze metrics more than in gross reading outcomes.

**Effect direction & size.** Size effect on fixation behavior; type effect small/mixed.

**Limitations/caveats.** 2008 screens; specific fonts; eye-metrics may not map directly to comprehension.
