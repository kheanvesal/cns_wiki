# Effects of Display Factors on Chinese Proofreading Performance and Preferences

**Citation.** Chan, A. H. S., & Ng, A. W. Y. *Effects of Display Factors on Chinese Proofreading Performance and Preferences.* (Manuscript; City University of Hong Kong.) — source file is the revised clean-copy `.doc`.

**Reading task(s).** Proofreading (screen, Chinese).

**Question/aim.** How screen display factors affect Chinese proofreading performance and reader preferences.

**Method.** Experimental proofreading study (behavioral performance + subjective preference). Part of the Chan/CityU Chinese-proofreading program.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Display factors (typeface/size/spacing/layout family used across this group's work).

**Key findings.**
- Display factors affect both objective proofreading performance and subjective preference.
- Objective-best settings and preferred settings need not coincide (a recurring performance-vs-preference tension).

**Effect direction & size.** Manuscript; consult file for specifics.
