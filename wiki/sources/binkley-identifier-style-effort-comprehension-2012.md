# The Impact of Identifier Style on Effort and Comprehension

**Citation.** Binkley, D., Davis, M., Lawrie, D., Maletic, J. I., Morrell, C., & Sharif, B. (2013). The impact of identifier style on effort and comprehension. *Empirical Software Engineering*, 18, 219–276.

**Reading task(s).** Comprehension (source-code reading — a specialized reading domain).

**Question/aim.** Whether identifier style — **camelCase** vs. **under_score** — affects speed and accuracy of comprehending source code.

**Method.** A family of five studies; behavioral (speed, accuracy) plus eye-tracking in later studies; both novices and experienced developers.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Identifier tokenization style: camel case vs. underscore.

**Key findings.**
- Style affects speed and accuracy of reading/comprehending identifiers.
- Underscore tends to support faster/more accurate finding; camel case can be faster once trained (training/experience moderates).
- Effects interact with reader experience — practice shifts the advantage.

**Effect direction & size.** Style effects significant but moderated by training/experience; see paper for per-study magnitudes.

**Limitations/caveats.** Domain-specific (code, not prose); results depend on prior exposure to a style.
