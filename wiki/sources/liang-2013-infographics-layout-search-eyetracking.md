# The Effectiveness of Infographics Layout in Information Searching and Scanning: An Eye Tracking Study

**Citation.** Liang, Z. (2013). *The Effectiveness of Infographics Layout in Information Searching and Scanning: An Eye Tracking Study.* MSc thesis, Industrial Engineering, The Pennsylvania State University.

**Reading task(s).** Search + scanning (infographics).

**Question/aim.** How infographic layout (**linear vs. radial**) affects information acquisition at two levels: (1) perception, (2) interpretation requiring value comparison.

**Method.** Eye-tracking; N=40; metrics incl. time to fixate last of multiple targets, total fixation duration.

**Style/layout variables manipulated.** Infographic layout: linear vs. radial arrangement.

**Key findings.**
- **Level 1 (perception):** linear ≈ radial — equally effective for simple location.
- **Level 2 (comparison of values):** **radial more effective** than linear (e.g., time to last target t=2.52, p=0.018).
- Best layout depends on the cognitive demand of the search task.

**Effect direction & size.** Radial > linear for comparison-type search (significant); null at simple perception level.

**Limitations/caveats.** MSc thesis; N=40; specific infographic set; task-level interaction is the key nuance.
