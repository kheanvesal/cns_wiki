# University of Freiburg

Cognitive Science, Freiburg, Germany. Center for Cognitive Science eye-tracking laboratory.

## Role
Home of Beck & Konieczny's rhythm/subvocalization program ([[sources/rhythmic-subvocalization-poetry-eye-tracking]]), conducted spring/summer 2019 with the SR Research **EyeLink 1000** at 500 Hz.

## Notable
- The lab hosts a **long-stimulus** reading tradition — the study deliberately uses whole poems across multiple screens/pages rather than short sentence-on-sentence stimuli, which is why [[sources/rhythmic-subvocalization-poetry-eye-tracking]] contributes a method (staged main-model/complete-model analysis) useful well beyond verse.
- Participant recruitment via the departmental **Sona System** pool plus non-university channels (art associations, House of Literature Freiburg, University of Music), yielding a 38-participant sample aged 19–76 — unusual age spread for an eye-tracking study and a reminder that age is a confound even when unmodeled.
