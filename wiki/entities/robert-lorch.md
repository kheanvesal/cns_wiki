# Robert F. Lorch Jr.

**Who.** Robert F. Lorch Jr. (University of Kentucky) and collaborators (Lemarié, Péry-Woodley, Hyönä, Kaakinen). Program on **signaling devices, headings, and text structure** in expository reading.

**Corpus sources.**
- [[sources/lemarie-lorch-perywoodley-2012-headings]] — SARA framework of headings/signaling.
- [[sources/functional-headings-selective-attention]] — functional vs. content headings on attention.
- [[sources/hyona-lorch-kaakinen-2002-reading-to-summarize]] — eye-fixation reader strategies for summarizing.

**Contribution.** Headings/signaling carry both content and functional/structural information; they guide selective attention and macrostructure building — central to skimming/selective reading.
