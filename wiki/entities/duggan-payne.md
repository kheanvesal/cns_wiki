# Duggan & Payne

**Who.** Geoffrey B. Duggan & Stephen J. Payne (University of Bath). Foundational work on **skim reading as satisficing/foraging**.

**Corpus sources.**
- [[sources/duggan-payne-2009-text-skimming]] — *Text skimming: foraging under time pressure* (JEP:Applied). (3 file copies in raw.)
- [[sources/duggan-payne-2011-skim-satisficing]] — *Skim reading by satisficing: evidence from eye tracking* (CHI). (2 file copies.)

**Contribution.** Established that skimming effectively extracts **main points** by allocating attention to high-value text and stopping at a **satisficing** threshold — the mechanistic core of the [[syntheses/Skimming]] hub.
