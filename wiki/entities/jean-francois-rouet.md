# Jean-François Rouet

**Who.** Jean-François Rouet (CNRS / University of Poitiers) with Britt, Durik, Vidal-Abarca. Work on **task-driven reading, search tasks, and situated comprehension** (RESOLV tradition).

**Corpus sources.**
- [[sources/britt-et-al-dp-2022-r1]] — comprehension as a situated, goal-driven activity.
- [[sources/rouet-2002-search-tasks-comprehension]] — search tasks reshape comprehension (selective, relevance-driven).

**Contribution.** Formalizes how reader **goals and task context** govern reading depth/strategy — the moderator that explains style×task interactions across all four hubs.
