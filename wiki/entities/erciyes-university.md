# Erciyes University

Kayseri, Türkiye. Origin of the EOG/dyslexia-detection research program.

## Role
- Departments of **Biomedical Engineering** and **Child and Adolescent Psychiatry** produced [[sources/dyslexianet-eog-deep-learning-dyslexia-detection]] (İleri, Altıntop, Latifoğlu & Demirci, 2025), which spans signal acquisition, machine learning, and clinical diagnosis — an unusual and productive combination.
- Ethics approval file **2018/565** (approved 7 November 2018) for the EOG reading studies.
- Funded by TÜBİTAK grant **119E055**.

## Notable
One of the few research groups using **EOG rather than video eye tracking** for readability work — see [[concepts/eog-and-physiological-signals]]. The low-cost, deployable signal is an explicit goal: the authors frame the method as suitable for real-time, non-invasive school and clinic use. That framing is ambitious relative to their validation design (see [[entities/dyslexianet]] for the leakage caveat).
