# Readability Matters

Advocacy organization focused on making written information accessible, particularly for people with cognitive, learning, and visual disabilities. Co-authors on [[sources/readability-research-an-interdisciplinary-approach]] (via Kathy Crowley and Marjorie Jordan).

## Role in the corpus
Represents the **applied/practice** pole in a multi-stakeholder readability effort that also spans academic labs (Virtual Readability Lab, UCF), industry (Adobe, Sonos, ReadWorks), and non-profit technology (Seleggo-related). Its presence in the author list signals the paper's dual purpose: a research framework *and* a call to practice.

## Stakes represented
- Plain-language and plain-format communication for people who cannot rely on standard prose presentation.
- Downstream of the same premise as [[concepts/individual-differences-in-readability]]: format should be fitted to the reader, not assumed.
- Related practice areas: plain language, Easy Read, accessible web content, and assistive text adjustment.

## Caveat for wiki use
Advocacy organizations in a multi-disciplinary author list can carry implementation incentives. The framework paper itself notes the commercial and advocacy landscape; readers evaluating its claims should weigh that its normative recommendations ("we call on a community to…") are partly agenda-setting rather than empirical.
