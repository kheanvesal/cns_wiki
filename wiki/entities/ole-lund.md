# Ole Lund

**Who.** Ole Lund (University of Reading). Meta-scientific critic of legibility research.

**Corpus source.** [[sources/lund-1999-knowledge-construction-typography]] — PhD thesis on how legibility "knowledge" (esp. serif vs. sans) was constructed.

**Contribution.** Argues much received legibility wisdom rests on **weak/confounded methodology**; the serif-advantage claim is underdetermined. Pairs with Richardson's later review.
