# Miles A. Tinker

**Who.** Miles A. Tinker (University of Minnesota), with D. G. Paterson — the classic mid-century experimental program on **print legibility**.

**Corpus source.** [[sources/tinker-1963-legibility-of-print]] — *Legibility of Print* (1963).

**Contribution.** Established baseline legibility optima (type size, line length, leading; all-caps slower) via large speed-of-reading experiments. The reference point later screen-era work (Dyson, Richardson, Lund) revisits and qualifies.

**Caveat.** Pre-screen, print-era; speed/legibility focus, not deep comprehension.
