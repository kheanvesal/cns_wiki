# IRCCS E. Medea

Scientific Institute for the Study and Treatment of Neurological Disorders, Bosisio Parini, Lombardy, Italy. Clinical research partner in [[sources/validating-personalized-visual-auditory-parameters-dyslexia]] (Lorusso et al., 2024).

## Role
Co-author institution alongside **Seleggo NPO** (Besozzo) and **Betterdays Ltd.** (Milan) on the Seleggo validation study. E. Medea supplied the clinical population and the reading-deficiency assessment framing.

## Notable
The study recruited **49 children with atypical reading development** (mix of diagnosed specific reading disorder and special-educational-needs children) and **29 typical controls**, ages 8–14. The authors are careful to state that because the AD group was assembled *irrespective of diagnosis*, results should not be read as dyslexia-specific but as extending to children with reading disorders generally.
