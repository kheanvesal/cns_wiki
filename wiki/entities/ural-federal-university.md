# Ural Federal University — Dept. of Printing Arts and Web-Design

**What.** Yekaterinburg, Russia. Home of Tarasov, Sergeeva & Filimonova, whose group works at the seam of **printing arts / typographic technology and reading science**. Notable for treating legibility as a *measurement* problem as well as a design one.

**Why it matters.** The only corpus group that has argued for formal **standardization of typographic measurement** — x-height in millimetres as the sole typeface measure, line spacing as a fraction of x-height, lighting per ISO 3664:2009, fixed viewing distance. That position is a direct response to the field's reproducibility problem, not merely another readability study.

**Lab / line of work.**
- Psychophysical measurement of text stimuli under controlled light (e.g. a "universal light booth" as a standard light source, Tarasov et al. 2012).
- A proposed research methodology built on that booth (Tarasov et al. 2013).
- Textbook legibility and visual word recognition; the review in [[sources/tarasov-legibility-of-textbooks-2015]] diagnoses the field's contradictory results as caused by the absence of a unified approach.
