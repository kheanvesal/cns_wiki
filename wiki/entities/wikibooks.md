# Wikibooks

Free online textbooks and study guides, used as the **material source** in [[sources/adaptive-personalization-educational-readings-simulated]] (Woo et al., 2026).

## Role in that study
- Learning-objective and knowledge-component ontologies were built from Wikibooks open textbooks, then curated in a browser-based **Ontology Atlas**.
- Textbook chunks were labeled with ontology entities, and aligned reading–assessment pairs generated from those labels.
- Three subject ontologies were sampled: computer science, inorganic chemistry, general biology — the basis for the domain-dependent result (adaptive reading significantly helped CS, was inconclusive for chemistry, neutral-to-slightly-negative for biology).

## Caveats as a corpus
- Single-source, English-language, open-license — not a representative curriculum.
- Open textbooks are simplified relative to standard course materials, so readability and knowledge-component density differ from what real learners encounter.
- Ontology curation from it is a manual/curated step whose inter-rater reliability is not reported.
