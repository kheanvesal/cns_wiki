# Virtual Readability Lab

University of Central Florida (Orlando). Readability/typography research lab; several members are authors on [[sources/readability-research-an-interdisciplinary-approach]] and [[sources/accelerating-adult-readers-typeface]].

## Role
A named institutional node in the multi-disciplinary readability framework paper, positioned alongside industry (Adobe), advocacy organizations, and commercial accessibility firms. The lab's Ben D. Sawyer is the corresponding author of the framework paper and a co-author on the adult-typeface study.

## Program themes
- Individual differences in readability — the 51% within-person font variance result originates here.
- Font/typeface and reading speed; the "preference does not predict effectiveness" finding.
- Methodology for readability testing (materials, participants, licensing constraints).
- Bridging academic psycholinguistics with commercial text-rendering.
