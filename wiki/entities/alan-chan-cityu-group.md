# Alan H. S. Chan / CityU HK Group

**Who.** Alan H. S. Chan and collaborators (Tsang, Ng, So) at **City University of Hong Kong**; a sustained program on **display factors in on-screen Chinese proofreading/reading**.

**Corpus sources.**
- [[sources/chan-2014-line-length-spacing-proofreading]] — line length/number/spacing × proofreading + scrolling.
- [[sources/chan-2011-optimum-interface-chinese-proofreading]] — optimum interface design + Chinese Proofreading System.
- [[sources/chan-ng-display-factors-chinese-proofreading]] — display factors × performance/preferences (manuscript).
- [[sources/huang-2017-line-spacing-simplified-chinese]] — eye-tracking line spacing (CityU student, same milieu).

**Recurring themes.** Line spacing/number as strong levers; scrolling as a distinct cost; performance-vs-preference divergence; comparison vs. noncomparison proofreading.

**Caveat for synthesis.** Evidence is **Chinese-script specific** — generalization to alphabetic scripts is untested. Flag when generalizing proofreading layout claims.
