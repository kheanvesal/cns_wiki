Gen-AI content-layer specimen sheet

The corpus's third layer — content operations an LLM performs on text, distinct from both text-style (glyph) and layout (spatial/structural). Same demonstration format as the other two sheets: a real passage rewritten, tagged with the wiki's verdict.

Tags: green = helps/neutral · amber = can hurt / contested · blue = emerging / metric-validated only · gray = untested for reading performance. Companion to text-style-specimens.html + layout-pattern-specimens.html; see [[concepts/text-style-vs-layout]] "content → layout → text style" stack and [[syntheses/gen-ai-and-reading]].

Base passage used across panels 1–3: "The mitochondrion is a membrane-bound organelle that generates most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate, used as a source of chemical energy."

Simplification (rewrite, content preserved)helps/neutral readability
original

The mitochondrion is a membrane-bound organelle that generates most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate, used as a source of chemical energy.

LLM-simplified (vocabulary + grammar; content kept)

Mitochondria are tiny parts inside cells. They make most of the energy a cell needs, in the form of a molecule called ATP.

Improves Flesch-Kincaid readability without hindering learning, N=37 [[sources/pascoal-2026-llm-readability-student-comprehension]]. Naïve prompts can still worsen text if unguided [[sources/hedlin-2025-prompting-readability-chatgpt]].
Summarization / compression (content removed)can hurt durable comprehension
original

The mitochondrion is a membrane-bound organelle that generates most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate, used as a source of chemical energy.

LLM summary (detail deleted, not reworded)

Mitochondria make energy for cells.

Rates as good as human summaries yet comprehends worse; auto-metrics fail to track this [[sources/guo-2025-llm-plain-language-summaries]]. Hurts strong readers disproportionately [[sources/rolle-2025-gpt-tools-comprehension]]; underperforms note-taking for retention [[sources/kreijkes-2026-llm-notetaking-comprehension]].
Personalization / level adaptationNLP-metric validated only
beginner-level target

A mitochondrion is a tiny part inside a cell. It makes energy the cell can use.

advanced-level target

Mitochondria are membrane-bound organelles that synthesize the bulk of a cell's ATP via oxidative phosphorylation.

Validated by NLP adaptation metrics, not reader comprehension outcomes — an open question [[sources/mcnamara-2025-genai-text-personalization]].
Prompt strategy for simplificationnaïve prompt can worsen text
naïve prompt ("make this easier")

Mitochondria are membrane-bound organelles generating cellular ATP — essentially the cell's power generators, biochemically speaking.

metric-guided prompt (target grade level)

Mitochondria are small parts inside cells. They make energy the cell needs to work.

Metric-Guided Prompt significantly reduces Flesch-Kincaid grade level vs a standard/naïve prompt, which can leave text unchanged or harder [[sources/hedlin-2025-prompting-readability-chatgpt]].
